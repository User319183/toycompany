Our Journey

2010

How We Started

Little Bird Toys began in a small workshop behind our family home. What started as a hobby crafting wooden toys for our own children quickly grew into something more when friends and family began requesting custom pieces.

Founded by master woodworker James Thompson and his wife Emma, our passion for quality toys that inspire creativity became our driving force.

Present Day

Our Process

Each toy begins as a sketch, inspired by both classic designs and modern play patterns. Our craftspeople transform these sketches into prototypes that undergo rigorous testing for safety, durability, and play value.

Our Core Values

These principles guide everything we do - from how we source materials to how we design and craft each toy. They're the foundation of our company culture.

Sustainability

We're committed to eco-friendly practices at every step. Our woods come from responsibly managed forests, and we've implemented a zero-waste initiative in our workshop, repurposing scraps into smaller toys or heating our facility.

FSC Certified Zero Waste

Child Development

Each toy is thoughtfully designed with input from early childhood educators to foster cognitive development, fine motor skills, problem-solving abilities, and imaginative play. We believe the best toys grow with your child.

Educational Age-Appropriate

Quality & Safety

Safety is our top priority. Every toy undergoes rigorous testing that exceeds regulatory standards. We use only non-toxic, child-safe finishes, and our designs eliminate small parts and sharp edges to create heirloom-quality toys.

ASTM Certified Non-Toxic

Community Impact

For every toy sold, we donate one to children in need through our "Play It Forward" program. We've provided over 5,000 wooden toys to children's hospitals and community centers since our founding.
